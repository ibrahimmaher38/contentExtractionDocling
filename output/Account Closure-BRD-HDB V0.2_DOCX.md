## Account Closure - Process Requirement

This Process handle the full cycle of Account Closure requested by the Customers to Close his Account.

## Hierarchy of Process Teams

The departments and teams engaged in the documentary workflow and their approval hierarchies

| **Group / Team**                | **Maker**   | **Checker**   | Manager   |
|---------------------------------|-------------|---------------|-----------|
| Branch Customer Service         | Y           | Y             | N/A       |
| Central Unit of Account Opening | Y           | Y             | N/A       |

## Requirements of Participants Groups &amp; Roles

| Category                         | Requirement                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| User/Group Specific Requirements | Group / Role Requirement  Branch Customer Service Maker Search Customer and Initiate Account Closure request if the customer is existing. Branch Customer Service Checker Review the submitted Request by Same branch Customer service Maker. Central Unit Maker Review the request and the attached documents and submit it to his checker for approve. Central Unit Checker Review the request and in case the request is approved the account will be Closed on core banking system. |

## Process Users Roles &amp; Teams requirements

| Central Unit Department     | The received tasks will be added automatically to checkers include conditions:  - 1- Approve the task and end the process - 2- Return the task to Branch customer service maker. | Central Unit Department Checker/Head of Central Unit |

## Assumptions and Constraints

Account Closure Process applied as a for any customer wants to close his account on the following assumptions:

- The authorized users can use searching capabilities in the BPM system for the completed tasks and / or completed process within the user’s assigned branch and view the process instance data and its attachments.
- The authorized Central unit users can use searching capabilities in the BPM system for the completed tasks and / or completed process for all transactions on the systems produced from all branches and sectors and view the process instance data and its attachments.
- Any performance report can be generated from BPM system as long as the data is available in our system.
- Any searching query can be defined from the BPM system as long as the metadata of this query is available on the system.
- All interfaces will be in both Arabic and English languages.
